Case Study About Marketing Approach

A strategic look into Conversion Goals, Google Ads, and Web Analytics optimization.

Conversion Goals & Objectives

Primary Goal:
  • Generate qualified leads (form fills, demo bookings, Free-Trial).
  • Maximize conversions, add to cart, and grow revenue.
  • Track conversions and CTA clicks via GTM & GA4.
Secondary Goals:
  • Enhance local brand awareness.
  • Boost website traffic.

Marketing Approach – Google Ads

Why Run PPC Ads?
  • Immediate Visibility vs. Slow SEO – Reach users faster than SEO allows.
  • Target High-Intent Users – Paid search captures users already looking for solutions like “AI social media engagement tool”.
  • Precise Budget Control – Set daily cost, pause anytime, and track exact CPA.
  • Data-Driven Decisions – Get real-time conversion insights with GTM + GA4.
Campaign and Conversion Tracking Strategy
  • Keyword research using Google Keyword Planner & SEMrush.
  • Ad structure by branded and non-branded keywords.
  • Responsive Search Ads with 10–12 headlines & 3–4 descriptions.
  • Use CTAs like “Book Demo”, “Try Free”, “Add to Cart”.
  • Extensions: Sitelinks, Callouts, Structured Snippets.
Bidding Strategy:
  • Phase 1: Maximize Conversions (data collection).
  • Phase 2: Target CPA ($15–20).
  • Budget Split: Search (70%), PMax (20%), Remarketing (10%).

Conversion Tracking & Metrics

Tracking ensures ads optimize for leads, not just clicks. Key setups include:

  • GTM container setup on website.
  • Track form submissions, CTA clicks, and scroll depth.
  • Push events to GA4 and import into Google Ads.

Key Metrics: Target CPA ($15–20), CPC ($1.5–2.5), CTR (4–7%), CVR (7–10%).

Web Analytics & CRO Approach

  • Enable Enhanced Measurement in GA4 (page views, scrolls, clicks).
  • Create custom events (form_submit, CTA_click_demo, CTA_click_trial).
  • Link GA4 to Google Ads for conversion imports and remarketing audiences.
User Behavior Tracking

Use tools like Hotjar or Microsoft Clarity to analyze user interactions and drop-offs.

A/B Testing

Test headlines, CTAs, layouts, and images to double conversion rates.

Analytics & Reporting Dashboards

Build GA4/Looker dashboards to track conversions, bounce rates, and engagement trends over time.

Competitor Analysis & Summary

Compare monthly traffic, paid vs organic results, and offers with competitors to refine marketing and content strategy.
